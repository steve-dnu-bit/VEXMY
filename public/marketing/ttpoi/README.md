# Tap to Pay on iPhone — Marketing Toolkit assets

Drop Apple Marketing Toolkit exports here before general availability marketing.

## Required for Apple §6

| File | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| `hero.jpg` (or `.png`) | In-app splash Hero (requirement 6.2 / 3.2). Referenced by `TapToPayAwarenessSplash`. |
| `launch-email-banner.jpg` | Optional banner for the Launch email (6.1). |

## Access

Marketing Guide & Toolkit access page password is in Apple’s App Requirements PDF (do not share publicly).

## Rules

- Use only Apple-approved Toolkit templates (light customization: logo, brand colors, fonts).
- Do **not** invent illustrations of iPhone or Tap to Pay on iPhone.
- Always say **Tap to Pay on iPhone** in full — never shorten to “Tap to Pay” alone in marketing, and never put the phrase in the **app name**.
- Wait for full general availability before external launch email / push marketing waves (in-app education and awareness are allowed earlier for entitlement review).

Until assets are present, the in-app splash uses a text-first shell that still brands **Tap to Pay on iPhone**.
